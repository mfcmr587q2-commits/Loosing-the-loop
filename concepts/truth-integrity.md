# Truth / Integrity

Truth is treated here as an epistemic constraint, not as a claim that a system is infallible.

The integrity layer should preserve the distinction between what was observed, what was inferred, what is hypothesized, and what remains unknown.

## Minimum record

```text
claim
source / provenance
observation time
confidence
uncertainty
counterevidence
transformations
validator
authorization state
```

The goal is to make it harder for a system to convert an unsupported internal belief into an apparently authoritative fact.
